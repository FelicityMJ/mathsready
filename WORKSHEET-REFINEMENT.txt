REFINED
-------
• Official fluency mastery sets now show the whole set as a worksheet grid.

• Going beyond SMT does not stop the sheet; the bar stays red and the child finishes accurately.

• Parent Worksheet tab can open parent-overseen worksheet practice or create a printable fluency sheet.
• Existing broader V6 printable skill worksheet remains available.
