SpeedyMaths V6.33 — Admin Lock + Maths Snake progression

ADMIN
- Admin section now asks the signed-in owner to confirm their Firebase account password.
- Password is reauthenticated directly with Firebase Authentication; no admin password is hard-coded into index.html.
- Admin unlock lasts up to 15 minutes, then relocks.
- A "Lock Admin" button lets the owner lock it immediately.
- Existing Firebase owner/admin authorization and Firestore rules remain in place underneath the password gate.

MATHS SNAKE
- Losing a wall/tail life now removes at most one earned snake square instead of resetting the snake to its starting length.
- Monster life loss also removes at most one earned square.
- Wrong maths answers shrink the snake by one square maximum and never below the 3-square starter length.

- Additional skin colours added for the in-between unlocks.
