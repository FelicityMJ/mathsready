NAT5 READY MATHS — V6.8 FACE-OFF LIVE SYNC
=============================================

WHAT THIS FIXES
---------------
• Players joining a Face-Off room now appear live on the host and other joined devices.
• No browser refresh should be needed to see a new player.
• When the host starts a round, every open participant device reacts automatically.
• Every round now has a shared 10-second ready countdown.
• The countdown shows a large 10 → 1 display plus a filling progress bar.
• At the end of the countdown, Question 1 opens automatically.
• The child's race timer starts only when the questions actually open, not during the ready countdown.
• This applies to round 1, later rounds and rematches.

DEPLOYMENT
----------
1. Replace the root index.html in the GitHub mathsready repository with this index.html.
2. Commit to main and wait for GitHub Pages to redeploy.
3. Hard refresh both devices before testing (Ctrl+F5 on desktop; reload/close and reopen on mobile).

FIREBASE RULES
--------------
No Firestore rules change is required for V6.8 if the V6.7 rules are already published.

RECOMMENDED TEST
----------------
1. Open Family A on one device and Family B on another.
2. Family A creates a Face-Off and stays in the lobby.
3. Family B joins with the MATH code.
4. Confirm Family A sees Family B appear without refreshing.
5. Host presses Start round 1.
6. Confirm both devices show the 10-second countdown automatically.
7. Confirm both devices open Question 1 automatically when the countdown ends.
