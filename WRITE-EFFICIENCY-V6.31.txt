MathsReady V6.31 — Firestore write-efficiency update

Purpose
-------
Reduce unnecessary Firestore writes without changing the learner-facing experience.

Changes
-------
1. Ordinary child-progress saves are batched for up to ~15 seconds while activity continues.
2. Completed practice sessions, Beat Your Best, Fluency mastery, Fluency helper and Arcade results force an immediate save.
3. Visibility/page-exit flushes only children with genuinely unsaved changes, with duplicate flush protection.
4. Public mastery leaderboard documents are only written/deleted when that specific leaderboard entry has changed.
   A small per-browser localStorage signature cache prevents repeat writes of unchanged leaderboard results.
5. Face-Off live-progress writes are throttled to approximately every 3 answers or 2.5 seconds, while final results still save immediately.
6. Snake coin collection no longer writes to Firestore per coin; coins are persisted with the completed run/checkpoint save.
7. Existing Firestore rules/data model are unchanged.

Safety
------
- Learner state still updates immediately in memory/UI.
- Important completed results bypass the batching delay.
- Normal in-progress work gets periodic cloud checkpoints.
- Existing visibility/page-exit handlers flush dirty progress.

Developer diagnostic
--------------------
